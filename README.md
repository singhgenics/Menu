# Cabin East: Menu

Menu, crockery and plating guide for a Pan-Asian dim sum and craft-beer pub in Betalbatim, Goa. One page with four views:

- **Menu**: the full food and drinks menu.
- **Crockery**: every plate, bowl, glass and bar tool, with quantities for 26 covers, and which dish or drink goes in which vessel.
- **Plating**: a card for every dish and drink with the build order, garnish, serving temperature and a kitchen note.
- **Furnishings**: furniture, bar, lighting, fans, ACs, finishes, art and fittings for the Simplex room, as 70 buy-able lines with sizes, materials, quantities, positions and planning prices, two coded plans (furniture, and ceiling with lights, fans and ACs), a budget with phase 1 / phase 2, design checks, and a gallery of 40 interior and product images grouped by section.

Cabin East is the working name (trademark not cleared). Dish, crockery and plating images were generated with Google Flow as visual targets and show the style, not a specific supplier's product.

Open `index.html` in a browser; there is nothing to build or install.
